Project Description

A platform where DIY enthusiasts share and discover craft tutorials, buy supplies, and showcase their finished projects.

Problem Statement:

DIY and handcraft enthusiasts currently struggle with a fragmented experience where tutorials, required raw materials, and community feedback exist across entirely separate websites. Finding structured, step-by-step craft instructions often requires switching between video platforms and blogs, while sourcing the exact materials listed in a project leads to frustrating searches on unrelated e-commerce stores. Furthermore, creators lack a dedicated, cozy space to showcase their completed works and inspire fellow markets. Craftroom addresses this gap by offering an all-in-one platform where crafters can easily follow structured tutorials with difficulty ratings, purchase the exact supplies needed directly from the materials list, and share their finished projects within a supportive community.
